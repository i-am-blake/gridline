# Gridline

**Ride the beat. Chase the sun.**

Gridline is a neon, music-driven riding game that runs in the browser from a single HTML file. You ride a glowing vehicle down an endless grid toward a giant setting sun, and everything around you moves with the music: the sky breathes with the bass, the sun wears a spectrum crown that dances to every frequency, obstacles roll in on the beat, and the road's speed is locked to the tempo.

## How to play

Play it at **https://i-am-blake.github.io/gridline/**, or open `index.html` in a modern browser, pick a vehicle, world and beat, and press **Play**.

| Action | Desktop | Phone |
|---|---|---|
| Steer | ← → | Drag anywhere, or tilt the phone |
| Jump on flat ground | Space | Swipe up |
| Backflip / front flip (in the air) | ↓ / ↑ | Drag down / up, or hold ⟲ / ⟳ |
| Pause | P | ❚❚ |

- **Ramps and tunnels** score points and refill your gas. Chain them for a combo multiplier.
- **Flips** pay big, but land crooked and you wipe out.
- **Blocks, robots and lasers** cost a life.
- **Gas** burns as you ride. Hit enough jumps and tunnels to reach each checkpoint gate before you run dry.
- **A 15× combo** earns a force field that takes one hit for you.

## Vehicles

| Vehicle | Strength |
|---|---|
| Light Bike | Fastest top speed and the biggest boost from every landing |
| Hoverboard | Highest jumps and the quickest flips |
| Dune Buggy | Sharpest steering and the most lives; flips only off ramps and tunnels |

## Worlds

- **Sunset Grid**: the classic neon grid under a striped sun, with boulders tumbling off the mountains.
- **Neon City**: a rolling highway through downtown towers, overpasses, street lights and billboards.
- **Endless Void**: a winding, banking track floating through space, with the camera lifting overhead during the drop.

## Beats

- **Synthwave**: French filter-house energy with pumping chords and a robotic lead.
- **Lo-fi**: lazy, dusty beats with warm keys and rain in the quiet parts.
- **Microphone**: ride to whatever is playing around you.

Each song follows a full arrangement (intro, groove, build, drop, breakdown), shown on the timeline in the top right, and shifts key as you progress.

## Chapters

Every level is faster than the last. Every five levels opens a new chapter:

- A new sky: dusk, then night, then aurora.
- A new obstacle: patrol robots themed to each world (level 5), beat-synced lasers (level 10) and split roads (level 15).
- A **gauntlet** before each milestone, lined with red barrier rails. Clear it without a hit for a bonus.
- A reward at each milestone: an extra life or a free force field.
- **Weather** from level 3: rain, crosswinds, fog, snow and lightning storms, each changing how the road feels.

Scores go to a shared leaderboard, split by map, showing each rider's best score and the level they reached.

## More

- **Share your score**: after a run, tap *Share score* to send a neon score card and a link to friends.
- **Install it like an app**: on iPhone, open the game in Safari, tap Share ⬆ then *Add to Home Screen*. On Android, tap *Install app* on the home screen (or Chrome's menu → *Install app*). It then opens full screen and works offline.
- **Readable for every kind of colour vision**: hazards are always bright yellow with a bold X, and everything you want to hit is cyan or blue, so nothing depends on telling red from green or orange.
